# Professional Accomplishments & Impact Stories

This file stores your key achievements and impact narratives. These are used by Claude to enhance your tailored resumes by matching your accomplishments to job requirements.

**Format**: Each accomplishment includes:
- **Title**: Brief accomplishment name
- **Impact**: Quantified result (metrics, scale, timeframe)
- **Context**: Business problem or opportunity
- **Skills**: Core skills demonstrated
- **Keywords**: Technical/business terms to highlight

## How to Use These Accomplishments

When tailoring your resume for a job posting:

1. **Claude will automatically match** your accomplishments to job requirements
2. **Relevant accomplishments** will be woven into resume bullet points
3. **Keywords** from your accomplishments will reinforce posting keywords
4. **Impact metrics** will be highlighted to show quantified value

### Example
If a job posting emphasizes "data governance," Claude will pull:
- Built Data Governance & Compliance Framework
- Accomplishment: Defined Core Metrics Standard
- Keywords like: compliance, data security, audit-ready, access controls

## Notes

- These accomplishments are **factual and verifiable** - they're your genuine impact, not inflated claims
- They should be **specific with metrics** - "50% reduction" is better than "significant improvement"
- They span **multiple domains** - analytics, operations, leadership, technical, business impact
- They demonstrate **influence and impact** - not just tasks, but outcomes
- They use **job-relevant keywords** naturally - no keyword stuffing, just natural language
